What's inside

Lo-fi music for cozy games: late-night study, a Sunday morning, a rainy window seat, a cat nap, old polaroids, a bedroom desk, an autumn walk and a midnight snack. Jazzy chords, soft swung drums, tape warble and vinyl crackle, made to play for hours without getting tiring. Every file is an exact number of bars and loops seamlessly: set your audio player to loop and there is no gap or click. Reverb tails, rain, birds and vinyl crackle wrap around the loop point too.

Every track comes in two versions: the full track and a "-calm" version without the lead melody, made to sit under dialogue and menus. Both have the same length, arrangement and volume, so you can crossfade between them mid-game.

Formats (the same 16 files in each folder):
  wav/  44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo (best quality, loops perfectly everywhere)
  ogg/  Ogg Vorbis (small, loops perfectly in Godot, Unity, GameMaker and the web)
  mp3/  MP3 (for video and web; MP3 adds a tiny silence at the start, so loop the WAV or OGG in games)

Use it in Godot 4

Use the OGG files in Godot 4 and turn on looping; every track loops without a gap.

var music := AudioStreamPlayer.new()
add_child(music)
var song: AudioStreamOggVorbis = load("res://cozy-lofi-beats/ogg/autumn-walk-calm.ogg")
song.loop = true
music.stream = song
music.play()
