What's inside

Calm, warm music inspired by East Asian pentatonic melodies: a tea house, cherry blossoms, a zen garden, a lantern festival, a mountain shrine, a rainy temple, a koi pond and a village morning. Koto, a breathy bamboo-style flute, harp, kalimba, vibraphone, bells, acoustic piano, soft pads, rain and gentle percussion. Every file is an exact number of bars and loops seamlessly: set your audio player to loop and there is no gap or click. Reverb tails and rain wrap around the loop point too.

Every track comes in two versions: the full track and a "-calm" version without the lead melody, made to sit under dialogue and menus. Both have the same length, arrangement and volume, so you can crossfade between them mid-game.

Use it in Godot 4

Use the OGG files in Godot 4 and turn on looping; every track loops without a gap.

var music := AudioStreamPlayer.new()
add_child(music)
var song: AudioStreamOggVorbis = load("res://cozy-zen-music/ogg/cherry-blossoms-calm.ogg")
song.loop = true
music.stream = song
music.play()
