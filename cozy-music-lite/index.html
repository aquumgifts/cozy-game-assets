Use it in Godot 4

Use the OGG files in Godot 4 and turn on looping; every track loops without a gap.

var music := AudioStreamPlayer.new()
add_child(music)
var song: AudioStreamOggVorbis = load("res://cozy-music-lite/ogg/track.ogg")
song.loop = true
music.stream = song
music.play()
