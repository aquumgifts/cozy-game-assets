Cozy Nature Ambience — 12 Free Seamless Ambience Loops (Rain, Forest, Waves, Fire)

12 free 30-second ambience loops for cozy games: rain, storm, forest, river, waves, campfire, fireplace and more.

What's inside

Background ambience for cozy games: weather, nature and places. Each loop is 30 seconds and repeats with no gap or click (set your audio player to loop). Layer two or three (rain + fireplace, forest + river) to build your own scene.

Formats: wav/ (44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo) and ogg/ (Ogg Vorbis, loops perfectly in Godot, Unity, GameMaker and the web).

  Weather
    rain-light  (gentle rain, for cozy rooms and rainy days)
    rain-storm  (heavy rain with distant thunder)
    meadow-wind  (soft wind over a meadow, a few birds)
    winter-wind  (cold howling wind for snowy maps)
  Nature
    forest-day  (birdsong and rustling leaves)
    night-crickets  (crickets, a far owl, a calm night)
    river-stream  (a bubbling stream over stones)
    ocean-waves  (slow waves on a beach)
  Places
    cave-drips  (water drops echoing in a cave)
    campfire  (crackling campfire outdoors)
    fireplace-room  (fireplace and a ticking clock indoors)
    town-fountain  (a fountain in the town square, distant bell)

How they were made: synthesized by our own code from filtered noise, resonators and small events placed on a circular buffer, so the end flows into the start. No recorded samples.

Music that fits: Cozy Lofi Beats (aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-lofi-beats) and Cozy Farm Music (aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-farm-music). See LICENSE.txt for what you can do with these.

Use it in Godot 4

Play a sound once, with a tiny random pitch so repeats don't sound robotic.

var sfx := AudioStreamPlayer.new()
add_child(sfx)
sfx.stream = load("res://cozy-nature-ambience/ogg/campfire.ogg")
sfx.pitch_scale = randf_range(0.95, 1.05)
sfx.play()
