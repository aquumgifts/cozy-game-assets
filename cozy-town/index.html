What's inside

A cozy modern town, 16x16 top-down, same palette and 1px outline as Cozy Farm &mdash; the farm and the town are the same world.

What's inside (Complete)

  • 314 object sprites, 293 unique tiles and 1,104 character animation frames &mdash; 1,194 PNG files total, every one in 1x, 2x and 4x.
  • Ground: 221 tiles (185 unique) &mdash; asphalt and cobblestone streets, center lines, crosswalks, curb ramps, sidewalks, plaza paving, grass. Three 47-piece autotiles plus a 16-piece planter autotile.
  • Buildings: 6 buildings by day and by night &mdash; cafe, bakery, flower shop, bookstore, grocery, house &mdash; with lit windows and doors after dark.
  • Interiors: cafe, bakery and grocery &mdash; 108 floor/wall tiles and 123 furniture pieces.
  • Street props: 110 sprites &mdash; lampposts (on/off), benches, terrace tables and umbrellas, a kiosk and carts, bikes and scooters, a bus stop, traffic lights, signs, a fountain, planters and more.
  • Food & drink: 63 sprites, plus 6 street trees and bushes.
  • Characters: 4 playable characters with 8 animations in 4 directions each (idle, walk, run, carry, sit, wave, eat, water), and 10 neighbors with idle and walk in 4 directions.
  • Godot 4: a TileSet (ground + interiors) and SpriteFrames for every character, Tiled tilesets with wang sets for the autotiles, and a small demo project &mdash; walk down the main street and into the cafe.

Use it in Godot 4

Set Project Settings → Rendering → Textures → Default Texture Filter to Nearest for crisp pixels. Drop the PNGs into your project and use them as textures; for tiles, add the sheet to a TileSet with the tile size from the README.

$Sprite2D.texture = load("res://cozy-town/sprite.png")
