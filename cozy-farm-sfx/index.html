Use it in Godot 4

Play a sound once, with a tiny random pitch so repeats don't sound robotic.

var sfx := AudioStreamPlayer.new()
add_child(sfx)
sfx.stream = load("res://cozy-farm-sfx/ambience/ambience-morning-birds.wav")
sfx.pitch_scale = randf_range(0.95, 1.05)
sfx.play()
