Cozy Space Errands

Deliver four parcels around a cozy space station. A tiny top-down game for your browser, 3-5 minutes.

Download free on itch.ioPlay in your browser

About it

First job on the station: deliver the mail. Mechanic Gus has four parcels in the cargo bay. Walk the corridor, open the doors, find Rosa, Doctor Lin, Officer Noor and the captain, and hand them their things. Then try to beat your best time.

A tiny, relaxing top-down game (3 to 5 minutes) that plays in your browser, with keyboard or touch.

How to play

  • Arrows / WASD: walk (or tap where you want to go)
  • Walk into a door on the back wall to go through it
  • Walk up to someone to talk; Space, Enter or a tap moves the dialog on
  • M: mute

Made only from free asset packs

Every pixel and sound here comes from the Cozy Space set, free for your own games:

  • Cozy Space Interiors: the rooms, walls and sliding doors
  • Walking Crew: the crew, walking in four directions
  • Cozy Space Station: beds, consoles, plants and the rest of the furniture
  • Portraits and Pixel Font: the dialog
  • Icons & UI: the parcels and buttons
  • Music (Nebula Drift) and SFX: music and sounds

Want them all in one folder? Cozy Space Complete bundles every Cozy Space pack, plus a ready Tiled map and the master palette.

How it was made

One HTML file with plain JavaScript and a canvas, no engine. Art, music and sounds made with our own code.

More free games from the set: Cozy Space Shift (story), Cozy Space Run (shooter), Cozy Space Hop (platformer).

Play it and build on it

Play it in your browser at aquumgifts.github.io/cozy-space-errands/. The full source is on GitHub at github.com/aquumgifts/cozy-space-errands, so you can change the art and the text and make your own version.
