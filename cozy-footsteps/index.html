What's inside

Ten surfaces: grass, dirt path, wood floor, stone, snow, sand, shallow water, carpet, gravel and metal grate. Each has 5 different steps (step-<surface>-1..5: pick one at random on each step so walking never sounds repetitive) and a jump landing (land-<surface>).

Format: WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono, normalized; each step starts and ends in silence.

Use it in Godot 4

Play a sound once, with a tiny random pitch so repeats don't sound robotic.

var sfx := AudioStreamPlayer.new()
add_child(sfx)
sfx.stream = load("res://cozy-footsteps/sfx/land-carpet.wav")
sfx.pitch_scale = randf_range(0.95, 1.05)
sfx.play()
