Cozy Farm Homestead — Map-Ready Farm Tileset for RPG Maker MZ/MV [16x16]

A complete 16x16 farm tileset for RPG Maker MZ/MV: terrain, crops, fences, buildings and props.

Get it on itch.io · US$2.99

What's inside

Everything to build a cozy farm map: terrain, tilled fields and crops, fences, buildings and props, in the same palette, grid and warm 1 px outline as the rest of Cozy Farm. 487 PNG files (1x, 2x and 4x, nearest-neighbor, transparent).

Drawn on a clean 16 x 16 grid. Scales with nearest-neighbor to 48 x 48 (3x) for RPG Maker MZ / MV, or 32 x 32 (2x), with no blur and the grid intact. Autotiles use a 13-piece edge/corner layout, so ground shapes snap together; every piece sits on the grid, so you can cut and recombine freely.

ground/        grass (plain + flowers) and 13-piece autotiles over grass: soil, wet soil, path
               and animated water (3 frames). Edge is 3 px on the grass side, fits any rect or L.
farmland/      four tilling stages: farmland-tilled, farmland-dry, farmland-seeded, farmland-watered
crops/         wheat, corn, pumpkin — 4 growth stages each (seeds, sprout, growing, ready)
fences/        posts, rails, ends, corners, a cross and a gate (open + closed)
structures/    farmhouse, barn, chicken-coop, silo, gate-arch
props/         well, scarecrow, wheelbarrow, crate, barrel, sign, water-trough, shipping-bin, mailbox, beehive, basket, hay-bale, lantern
nature/        bush, berry-bush, rock, big-rock, flowers, stump, mushrooms, sapling, log-pile, oak, oak-autumn, apple-tree, pine
barn-interior/ barn-floor, barn-wall, hay-pile, feed-sack, milk-can, tool-rack  (the bonus interior set)
sheets/        every group packed on a 16 px grid (ground, farmland, fences, crops,
               structures, props, nature and the barn interior)
examples/      a full homestead and a barn interior built only from this pack

Water animates: play the 3 water frames at ~4 fps. Made by hand in code and text art (one letter per color), with a fixed palette and a warm 1 px outline. License: see LICENSE.txt (use in free and commercial games; do not resell or redistribute the assets on their own).

Use it in Godot 4

Set Project Settings → Rendering → Textures → Default Texture Filter to Nearest for crisp pixels. Drop the PNGs into your project and use them as textures; for tiles, add the sheet to a TileSet with the tile size from the README.

$Sprite2D.texture = load("res://cozy-farm-homestead/barn-interior/1x/barn-floor.png")
