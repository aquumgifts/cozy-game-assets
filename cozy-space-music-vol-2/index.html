What's inside

Warm background music for cozy games: lounges, farms, cafes, shops, rainy nights and save rooms. Every file is an exact number of bars and loops seamlessly: set your audio player to loop and there is no gap or click. Reverb tails, vinyl crackle and rain wrap around the loop point too.

Every track comes in two versions: the original and a "-flute" version with a flute playing the lead melody (same length and arrangement, so you can switch between them mid-game).

Formats (the same 16 files in each folder):
  wav/  44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo (best quality, loops perfectly everywhere)
  ogg/  Ogg Vorbis (small, loops perfectly in Godot, Unity, GameMaker and the web)
  mp3/  MP3 (for video and web; MP3 adds a tiny silence at the start, so loop the WAV or OGG in games)

Use it in Godot 4

Use the OGG files in Godot 4 and turn on looping; every track loops without a gap.

var music := AudioStreamPlayer.new()
add_child(music)
var song: AudioStreamOggVorbis = load("res://cozy-space-music-vol-2/ogg/cargo-bay-market-flute.ogg")
song.loop = true
music.stream = song
music.play()
