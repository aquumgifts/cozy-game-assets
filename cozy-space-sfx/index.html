What's inside

Short chiptune-style sound effects for sci-fi and arcade games: weapons, explosions, pickups, movement, damage, UI, station machinery and short jingles.

Format: WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono, normalized to a similar loudness (peaks under -1 dBFS). Every file starts and ends in silence, so they can be triggered back to back.

Use it in Godot 4

Play a sound once, with a tiny random pitch so repeats don't sound robotic.

var sfx := AudioStreamPlayer.new()
add_child(sfx)
sfx.stream = load("res://cozy-space-sfx/sfx/alarm.wav")
sfx.pitch_scale = randf_range(0.95, 1.05)
sfx.play()
