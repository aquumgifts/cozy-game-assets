What's inside

Short chiptune tracks for sci-fi and arcade games. Every file is an exact number of bars and loops seamlessly: set your audio player to loop and there is no gap or click.

Format: WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono, similar loudness.

How they were made: composed by a small sequencer we wrote (melody, bass, arpeggio, pad and drums) and synthesized from square, triangle, saw and sine oscillators and noise. No recorded samples.

Use it in Godot 4

Use the OGG files in Godot 4 and turn on looping; every track loops without a gap.

var music := AudioStreamPlayer.new()
add_child(music)
var song: AudioStreamOggVorbis = load("res://cozy-space-music/ogg/track.ogg")
song.loop = true
music.stream = song
music.play()
