Cozy Space Run

A tiny pixel art space shooter you can play in your browser, made only from free asset packs.

Download free on itch.ioPlay in your browser

About it

A small arcade space shooter that runs right here in your browser. Dodge plasma, grab coins and crystals, power up to a triple shot and take down the flagship that shows up every minute or so.

How to play

  • Move: arrow keys or WASD, or drag anywhere on the screen (works on phones)
  • Fire is automatic
  • Crystal: stronger shot (up to 3 levels) · Shield icon: 10 s bubble · Heart: +1 life
  • P or Esc to pause, M to mute

Made only from free asset packs

Every sprite, background, effect and HUD piece in this game comes from the Cozy Space packs, which you can download for free and use in your own games (commercial use allowed):

  • Cozy Space Ships: the player ship, enemies, lasers, plasma and explosions
  • Cozy Space Backdrops: the parallax stars, nebulas, planets and asteroids
  • Cozy Space FX: shields, sparks, coins, crystals, smoke and shockwaves
  • Cozy Space Icons & UI: the HUD panels, bars, icons and buttons
  • Cozy Space Pixel Font: all the text (title, HUD, menus)

All 16 packs in one place: The Cozy Space set.

How it was made

One HTML file with plain JavaScript and a canvas, no engine. Sounds are synthesized in the browser. The art was drawn by our own code with a fixed palette; no image-generation AI was used.

Music: the Asteroid Run loop from Cozy Space Music, 6 free chiptune loops you can use in your own games: https://aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-space-music

New: Cozy Space Shift, a 3-minute cozy story made with the same packs.

Making your own game with this art? Cozy Space Complete has all 15 asset packs, the Story Kit and two exclusive bonuses in one download: https://aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-space-complete

Play it and build on it

Play it in your browser at aquumgifts.github.io/cozy-space-run/. The full source is on GitHub at github.com/aquumgifts/cozy-space-run, so you can change the art and the text and make your own version.
